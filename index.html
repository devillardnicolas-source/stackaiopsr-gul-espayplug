Around an agent: what you bring, what Forest brings

An agent working on regulated cases needs more than a model. Forest's part is the execution: the business objects, the actions, the permissions, the approvals and the record. The rest stays with you and the vendors you choose.

PartWho supplies itExamples

People and agents, one record. Each agent acts under its own account and role, so the case shows which agent acted, next to the people who approved. When Forest dispatches a case to an agent, the run keeps each step and its result.

The unit of analysis is the regulated operational decision. A signal informs a case. Dispatch routes it to an actor: a person, a BPO agent, or an AI agent. The actor decides under a procedure. The decision runs through a tool on the write path and changes a record. Proof ties decision and action to an obligation.
